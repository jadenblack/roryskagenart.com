The post Facebook first appeared on Central Texas Murals by Rory Skagen.]]> Facebook approached us in 2015 to create a new 64′ long mural for a new floor they added to their office. Located in downtown Austin, TX this mural was painted off site and installed in one evening. This allowed the office to work without interruption while the mural was being produced, and get a fun surprise the morning after installation.

Metaphorically, this mural explores the ups and downs of the creative process, and some of Facebook’s key phrases.
