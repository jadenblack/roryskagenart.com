About

Rory Skagen has been working as a professional artist in Austin, Texas for the last 15 years. Cofounder and CEO for Blue Genie Art, Rory developed this commercial art company between 1999 and 2010. In 2010, Rory Skagen decided to sell the business and strike out on his own to concentrate mainly on 2D art. Also, cofounder and part owner of the Blue Genie Art Bazaar, Rory continues to be a part of this dynamic holiday art show.
