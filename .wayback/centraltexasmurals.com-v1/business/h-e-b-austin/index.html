H-E-B & Austin

To celebrate their 50th anniversary at this location on Oltorf Rd. and Congress Ave. in Austin, Texas, H-E-B supermarket had us paint this 30 foot long mural on the side of their building.

Included in the design is an idealized representation of the Skyline of Austin, and a bat . . . the unofficial city mascot.

This project was done in coordination with First Night Austin, a city program that supports the arts during New Years.

About

Rory Skagen has been working as a professional artist in Austin, Texas for the last 15 years. Cofounder and CEO for Blue Genie Art, Rory developed this commercial art company between 1999 and 2010. In 2010, Rory Skagen decided to sell the business and strike out on his own to concentrate mainly on 2D art. Also, cofounder and part owner of the Blue Genie Art Bazaar, Rory continues to be a part of this dynamic holiday art show.
