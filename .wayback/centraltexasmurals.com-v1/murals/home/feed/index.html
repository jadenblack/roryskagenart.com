The post Andy Roddick Mural first appeared on Central Texas Murals by Rory Skagen.]]> This mural was painted for tennis champion, Andy Roddick.

Andy wanted an inspirational mural for his home gym.

Andy’s interior decorator, Sandy Senter, called us, and after consultation and a design stage, this was the mural decided upon.

The mural includes Andy’s personal heroes, his foundation logo, and personal slogans.

For an even more personal touch, the lettering is a scaled version of Andy’s own handwriting.

When designing the mural considerations had to be made for the placement of the athletic and entertainment equipment.
